The Sith Order is a disciplined and immersive Roblox group built around strength, hierarchy, knowledge, and control. This portal serves as the Order’s public-facing command center, where visitors, recruits, and members can explore its divisions, access official resources, and understand the structure of the Order.

The Sith Order is an ancient order of Force-wielders devoted to the dark side of the Force. It is focused on Sith-themed gameplay, with Sith ranks, training sessions, leadership structures, and organized events shaped by discipline and teamwork.

Recruitment

To join The Sith Order, you must first request membership through the main Roblox group: The Sith Order Roblox Group. You must also be in our Discord server: Join Discord. Once you’ve applied, a Darth+ ranked member will review your request and accept you.
